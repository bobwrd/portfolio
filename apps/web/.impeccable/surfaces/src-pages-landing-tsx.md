# Surface brief: the whole portfolio site

Scope: every page (About, Site guide, Margin of Error writing, Research tools index and the six tools, OneBook, Ask, Tradewinds). Mode: Read for About, guide, writing and Ask; Operate inside the tools, OneBook and Tradewinds, where the world supplies type, chrome and colour but controls stay standard.

Audience: admissions readers and academic contacts on a laptop, deciding in a minute whether the work is real. Constraints: all content and data survive; copy is tightened, never invented; About keeps its paper format; each tool keeps its own colour; tests and routes keep working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: every section is issued like a paper in one working-paper series: series band, number, title, author, abstract, numbered contents. It refuses the portfolio default of a hero over project cards.

OWN-WORLD: white paper, near-black ink, warm grey hairline rules. A 6px series band above each title block in that section's colour (oxblood for the site and writing, each tool's own colour for its paper). Libre Caslon Text for titles and reading prose; the system sans for navigation, labels and tool UI. Indexes are numbered ruled rows, never cards. Blue underlined links, as on an academic homepage.

STORY: the reader sees one serious body of work, understands the single question, and opens the tool or paper that interests them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: thin top bar (name left, sections right); series band with "Working Paper Series, No. 00" and the date; title left at 56 to 64px with author line and keywords, a ruled abstract box right; a numbered contents list below.

FORM: working-paper cover sheets fused with a plain academic homepage, my list position 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, user steered toward the canon), seed:7e4d39c246aac913. Signature: the coloured series band and paper number that open every section.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
